Frequently Asked Questions

How does Movie Melody work?

Movie Melody suggests songs that match the atmosphere, genre and mood of selected movies.

Do I need an account?

No. You can browse movie suggestions without creating an account.

Is Movie Melody free?

Yes. Basic song recommendations are free for all users.

Can I suggest a movie?

Yes. You can use the contact form below to suggest a movie.

Where can I find the suggested songs?

You can find them on the Suggested Songs page, where each movie has a list of recommended songs.
