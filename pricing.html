Got a question?

Can I change my membership plan later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your membership at any time.

What happens if I reach my daily movie limit?

Free users can watch one movie per day, while Premium users can watch up to three movies per day.

Are song suggestions unlimited?

Unlimited song suggestions are available on Premium and Yearly Unlimited plans.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. You may cancel your subscription at any time without extra fees.

Is the Yearly Unlimited plan cheaper?

Yes. The yearly plan costs €36 per year, offering a discount compared to paying monthly.
